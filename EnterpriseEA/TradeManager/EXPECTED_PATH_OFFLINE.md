# Expected trade path observation (offline)

`ExpectedPath.mqh` is the first isolated Phase 4 Trade Manager slice. It consumes one caller-attested virtual fill and completed bars for one trade. It does not create a virtual order, simulate broker fills, close a trade, move stops, trail, call AI, or grant entry permission. The EA shell does not include it.

The source follows the master specification's `ENTERED → PROVING → WORKING → MATURE` lifecycle and emits review reasons for invalidated structure, stop touch, slow MFE with volatility decay, and horizon timeout. Its `TIMEOUT_REVIEW`, `UNDERPERFORMING_REVIEW_REQUIRED`, and `RUNNER_REVIEW_AT_HORIZON` results are observations, not execution actions. The caller must supply the setup profile, trade identity, entry fill, invalidation stop, closed-bar sequence, structure/volatility/session/news state, and a content fingerprint. All thresholds in `Tests/Fixtures/expected-path-v0.1.0.json` are synthetic research seeds, including 3/8/15 bars and the R progress thresholds; they are not approved SCALP or SWING trading settings.

Initial R is the absolute price distance from the attested fill to its invalidation stop. The module records maximum favorable/adverse excursion in R and the first completed bar that reaches 0.5R, 1R, or 2R. Those are **bar indices**, not exact intrabar timestamps. MFE/MAE money, points under a broker-specific quote convention, spreads, commissions, swaps and entry/exit slippage require a later instrument/broker adapter. A bar whose range touches the stop may also have touched a target; without tick order, the module preserves earlier excursions, marks `STOP_TOUCHED_REVIEW`, and does not invent an intrabar fill or final outcome.

Bars must be confirmed closed, contiguous in sequence, strictly later than the fill and prior bar, have valid OHLC geometry and data quality `OK`, and bind to the same trade/config. A duplicate requires the same sequence, timestamp, trade/config and caller-attested fingerprint. Conflicting or missing bars set status `UNKNOWN`; an adapter must replay from authoritative history before using the path again. The fingerprint is a caller attestation, not a cryptographic verification performed here.

The fixture's long path yields 1.2R MFE, 0.2R MAE, first 0.5R at bar 2, and first 1R at bar 4. Python tests also cover SHORT math, slow progress, timeout review, stop-touch ambiguity, duplicate/conflict, missing bars and invalid fill geometry. They validate the design oracle only. MetaEditor compilation, MQL result parity, Strategy Tester and paper behavior remain unverified.

Next Phase 4 work: a virtual order/fill ledger that models spread, slippage, partial fills, fees and account risk consistently; authoritative exit events; full management audit and restart replay; and separate SCALP/SWING profiles calibrated by observed outcomes. None of those gates is complete in this slice.
