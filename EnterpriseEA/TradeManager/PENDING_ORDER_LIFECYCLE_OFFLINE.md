# Pending order lifecycle prototype (offline)

`PendingOrderLifecycle.mqh` is an isolated Phase 4 state-machine proposal for a single caller-attested BUY/SELL LIMIT or STOP order. It is **not included by `EnterpriseEA.mq5`**, does not call trading/file/network APIs, and cannot place or cancel an MT5 order. It follows [REQ-021](../PENDING_ORDER_LIFECYCLE_SPEC.md) and the [implementation plan](../PENDING_ORDER_LIFECYCLE_PLAN.md). Telegram remains paused.

The seed binds candidate/setup/config/instrument identity, broker server, account fingerprint, order ticket, trade style, accepted time, three UTC deadlines, closed-bar wait limit, volume step and an attested initial risk reservation per lot. Every broker event must match that server/account/ticket. Initialization rejects missing broker expiration capability, already expired setup or a reservation above the supplied budget. The effective deadline is the earliest of setup, session and broker expiration. Fixture values (3 bars, 300 seconds and USD arithmetic) are synthetic research inputs, not approved trading policy.

`EEA_PendingObserve` counts only contiguous confirmed closed bars. `EEA_PendingObserveGuard` accepts caller-attested setup/risk/news/quote/config snapshots between bars. `EEA_PendingCheckDeadline` is intended for an independent timer so a quiet market still requests cancellation. Invalid snapshots or sequence gaps mark `PENDING_RACE_UNKNOWN`; they cannot release reserved risk. If the setup, policy or quote premise becomes invalid, or a deadline is reached, the state changes to `CANCEL_REQUIRED`.

`EEA_PendingApplyBrokerEvent` accounts for an attested fill, cancellation request or broker-confirmed cancellation/expiration. A partial fill leaves the original deadline and remaining reservation intact. A sent cancel request keeps that reservation. Only a broker-confirmed terminal event with matching remaining volume releases it, including an expiration discovered after the local timer was missed. If this EA sent a cancel request, its confirmation must match the correlation ID; an independently confirmed broker cancellation can arrive without one. A fill during cancellation or after the deadline is recorded and marked `BREACH_REVIEW`; after final broker confirmation, a newly reported fill forces `PENDING_RACE_UNKNOWN` for full reconciliation. The volume invariant is `requested = filled + cancelled + remaining`. `filled_initial_risk_money` is supplied by the caller; the prototype does **not** calculate real spread, slippage, commissions, broker P/L or FTMO headroom. The account-wide ledger must reconcile this order with all other exposure.

`Tests/Fixtures/pending-order-lifecycle-v0.1.0.json`, `pending_order_oracle.py` and `pending_order_tests.py` cover synthetic TC-046–051 examples. Python uses Decimal as a design oracle; the tests do not execute MQL. An [isolated checkpoint contract](../Storage/PENDING_ORDER_CHECKPOINT_OFFLINE.md) preserves state on paper and forces broker reconciliation after restart. An [audit contract](../Storage/PENDING_ORDER_AUDIT_OFFLINE.md) checks transition evidence and an append chain without file I/O. Before Phase 4 gate, implement authoritative order/deal/history adapter, actual expiration capability and price-distance validation, snapshot freshness/fingerprints, serialized checkpoint and durable transition audit, timer/OnTradeTransaction integration, event reconciliation after restart, and MQL compile/Strategy Tester/Paper parity. The adapter must attest broker account identity, order ticket, deal IDs and event ordering; the fixture strings alone do not prove them. No Paper/Live permission follows from these offline results.
